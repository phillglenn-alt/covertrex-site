Privacy notice

This explains what happens to your information when you visit covertrex.com or sign up for Covertrex emails. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Covertrex is run by [your full legal name, or company name and number], based in the United Kingdom. We are responsible for your information. Contact us any time at info@covertrex.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you sign up.
  • A record of your sign-up: the date and time, that you ticked the consent box, that you confirmed by email, and that you came from this page.
  • Basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type. This is logged automatically by our website host and our email provider when your browser talks to them, mainly for security.
  • Visit statistics: which pages are viewed, the site you came from, browser, device type and country. These are collected by Cloudflare Web Analytics without cookies and are only used as totals.
  • Email activity, such as whether our emails are opened or links clicked. Our email provider records this so we can see what is useful.

We do not ask for your name, date of birth, phone number, address or payment details.

Why we use it

To send you emails about Covertrex: news, drops and launch updates. We do this only because you asked us to (your consent). You can withdraw that consent at any time by unsubscribing. Technical data is used to keep the site and sign-up working and secure, and visit statistics help us understand how many people visit and improve the site. Both are in our legitimate interests.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Who else handles it

  • Klaviyo stores our email list and sends our emails for us. Klaviyo is based in the United States and protects UK data using approved safeguards, including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and UK transfer agreements. Klaviyo's privacy notice.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts this website and keeps short-lived server logs, which include IP addresses. GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to this website so we can see how many people come and which pages they view. It does not use cookies or store anything on your device, and it does not track you across other websites. It records the page you visited, the site you came from, your browser, device type and country, and how fast the page loaded. Cloudflare is based in the United States and protects UK data under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Workspace handles our email inbox, so if you email us, your message is stored there.

We only use these companies to run Covertrex. They are not allowed to use your information for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

  • If you never confirm your sign-up, we delete your email address within 30 days.
  • If you unsubscribe, we keep only your email address on a "do not contact" list, so we never email you by mistake. Ask us and we will delete that too.
  • If we stop sending emails, or you have not opened one in 2 years, we delete your details.

Age

You must be 13 or over to sign up. If we learn that someone younger has signed up, we delete their details.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics (see above) to count visits. It is cookie-free, stores nothing on your device and does not build a profile of you, so there is no cookie banner. If we ever add cookies or tracking that the law says needs your consent, we will ask first.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop using it, or limit how we use it
  • give you a copy to take elsewhere

You can unsubscribe from any email with one click. For anything else, email info@covertrex.com and we will reply within one month. It is free.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page. If the change is significant, we will email subscribers before it takes effect.
